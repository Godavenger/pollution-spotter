## Registration and selection

- Every team member must register **individually** through the [official registration portal](https://events.mlh.com/events/15462-hacktoberfest-hack-day-kolkata-x-aki-hacks/register), even when participating as a team.
- People without a team may register individually; the organizers may help with team formation.
- **Registration does not confirm selection.** The registration form says shortlisted participants will need to submit a PPT about their proposed hackathon idea as part of selection.
- After registering, participants are encouraged to join the [official AKI HACKS WhatsApp group](https://chat.whatsapp.com/CgEBeW6xJAF2Y83DKnZtFv) for updates, PPT instructions, deadlines, selection information and team coordination.

The public form asks for current status, institution/organization, year of study, GitHub profile, LinkedIn profile, whether the participant has a team, a previous project and their contribution, whether they have an idea, a brief idea description, agreement to event rules/Code of Conduct, and an optional referring community.
